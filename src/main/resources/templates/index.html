How CampusFin Works

Complete the tools step by step to create a more complete picture of college financial readiness.

Step 1

Estimate College Cost

Enter tuition, housing, food, transportation, scholarships, family support, and student income.

Step 2

Build a Monthly Budget

Determine whether monthly income can support expected living expenses and savings goals.

Step 3

Assess Financial Readiness

Evaluate knowledge of budgeting, college costs, scholarships, credit, debt, emergency savings, and saving habits.

Step 4

Compare Colleges

Add different colleges and compare the financial impact of each option side by side.

Step 5

Test What-If Scenarios

Change scholarships, costs, family support, student income, and expenses to explore alternative plans.

Step 6

Review Combined Analysis

Review your saved cost, budget, and readiness information together with CampusFin's decision-support analysis.

Transparent and Experimental Analysis

CampusFin combines traditional financial calculations with experimental decision-support techniques. The Financial Readiness machine-learning model currently uses synthetic training data. Combined financial-risk analysis uses transparent rule-based logic.
